U.S. leads Australia leads

For the first decade or so of this century, more Phase 1 clinical trials per million residents were run in the US than Australia.

But beginning in 2014, Australia took the lead and never looked back, driven largely by the speed with which these trials are run there.

When we swap the y-scale to look at absolute numbers of Phase 1 trials, the US is still ahead by far, but growth has leveled off over the past decade, whereas Australian trials have more than doubled in that time frame.

However, that’s not the end of the story. If we zoom in on Australia’s clinical trials…

…and break them down by sponsorship country, the largest share belongs to the US, followed by Australia, then China, then other countries.
